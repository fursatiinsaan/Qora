## 📑 Project Overview
**Qora AI** is an autonomous financial intelligence platform where three specialized Qwen agents debate every financial transaction in real time:
1. **The Prosecutor (Qwen):** Scans contracts, line items, and bank health to find every possible discrepancy.
2. **The Defender (Qwen):** Cross-references purchase order addendums, time zones, and merchant context to kill false alarms.
3. **The Judge (Qwen):** Renders a final evidence-backed ruling with cited contract clauses and exact dollar math.

Coupled with **Vendor Behavioral Fingerprinting** (catching 6-month slow-motion drift) and **Red Team Pen-Testing** (synthetic attacks against your own approval policies).
